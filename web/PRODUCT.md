# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Today: a solo YouTube Shorts creator (the owner of @CanvasFFyt) making about one "guess the car" Short a day, mostly on
a phone, often in the evening. Later: public, for other faceless car-quiz creators arriving for the first time.
The job: go from a car name to a finished, beat-synced Short with upload text in a few minutes, without thinking about
editing. First-time visitors need to understand that in one glance; returning users should skip straight to work.

## Product Purpose
CanvasFF Studio turns a car name, a few free photos (or uploads) and a royalty-free song into a 10-second quiz Short
rendered in the cloud, plus a ready-to-paste title, description, tags and pinned comment. Success: a Short made in
under two minutes of attention, every step obvious, nothing to configure unless wanted.

## Brand Personality
Modern, minimal, calm. Confident and quiet: the Shorts are loud, the tool is not. Emotional goal: "this is easy".

## Anti-references
- Cluttered single-page forms with everything visible at once (the previous version of this app).
- Side panels that show placeholders before there is anything to show.
- Marketing chrome inside a tool: feature chips, hero badges, slogans on every screen. One calm welcome screen on a
  first visit is the only exception.
- Neon "gamer" styling, glassmorphism, glowing gradients.

## Design Principles
1. One decision per screen: each step asks one thing and makes the next action obvious.
2. Defaults over settings: "Surprise me" is the default; customization is available, never required.
3. Show the result, not the machinery: the render animation appears only once Generate is pressed.
4. Phone first: thumb-reachable primary action, large touch targets, no horizontal scrolling.
5. Calm surface, loud output: restrained UI so the car photos and the finished Short carry the color.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls; visible focus states; full keyboard use; respect
prefers-reduced-motion (crossfades instead of movement); touch targets at least 44px.
